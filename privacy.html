Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how A-Cropper handles information when you use our website and tools.

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to A-Cropper. A-Cropper is a free, browser-based image cropping and editing tool designed to help users crop, resize, rotate, flip, edit, and remove solid-color backgrounds from images.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use A-Cropper and how we handle that information.

2. Images You Upload

Your images are processed directly in your browser. A-Cropper is designed so that images can be edited locally on your device without requiring you to upload them to our servers.

When you select an image, the image is loaded into your browser for processing. Depending on the features you use, your browser performs operations such as cropping, resizing, rotating, flipping, and removing selected background colors.

A-Cropper does not require an account or login to use these editing features.

3. Information We Collect

A-Cropper does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials in order to use the core image editing features.

We may receive limited technical information through hosting, analytics, advertising, or other third-party services used by the website, if such services are enabled.

Such information may include general information such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • General usage information
  • IP address or approximate location where provided by third-party services

4. Cookies and Local Storage

A-Cropper may use browser technologies such as local storage to remember certain preferences, such as your selected light or dark theme.

Local storage data is stored by your browser on your device and can generally be removed through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

A-Cropper may use third-party services and libraries to provide website functionality, fonts, icons, analytics, advertising, hosting, or other services.

These third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include content delivery networks, font providers, analytics providers, advertising networks, and website hosting services.

6. Advertising

If advertising is displayed on A-Cropper, advertisements may be provided by third-party advertising networks.

These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

A-Cropper does not control the privacy practices of independent advertising providers.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to provide a safe and reliable website. However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because A-Cropper is designed to process images in the browser, users can edit images without creating an account or intentionally sending their images to a server.

8. Children's Privacy

A-Cropper is a general-purpose online tool and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the core functionality of the service.

9. External Links

A-Cropper may contain links to external websites, including tutorial videos, social media pages, or other resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any external website you visit.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to A-Cropper, its features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last Updated" date.
